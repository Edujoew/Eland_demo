About Us

A Tradition of Learning in the Tea Highlands

Founded in 2010, Elland Academy has grown into one of Kericho's most trusted institutions, where every learner is known, challenged, and supported.

Our Mission

To provide holistic, learner-centred education that unlocks every child's potential and prepares them for a changing world.

Our Vision

To be the leading centre of academic and moral excellence in the Rift Valley and beyond.

Core Values

Integrity, discipline, respect, teamwork, innovation, and service to community.

Message from the Principal

"At Elland Academy we believe every child is born with a gift. Our work is to discover it, polish it, and send it into the world with confidence and character. We invite you to visit, see our classrooms, and meet the teachers who will walk with your child."

Mrs. Grace Chebet
Principal, Elland Academy

Academics

Learning That Goes Beyond the Classroom

A full CBC curriculum from Pre-Primary to Junior Secondary with enrichment in sports, arts, and technology.

Pre-Primary

PP1–PP2. Play-based learning, early literacy, numeracy and creative expression.

Lower Primary

Grades 1–3. Strong foundation in English, Kiswahili, Mathematics and Environmental Activities.

Upper Primary

Grades 4–6. Integrated science, social studies, agriculture and creative arts.

Junior Secondary

Grades 7–9. Pre-technical studies, ICT, languages and pathway guidance.

⚽ Co-Curricular

Football, athletics, netball, volleyball, chess, drama, music & Scouts.

💻 Computer Lab

Modern lab with coding, typing and digital citizenship classes.

📖 Library & Clubs

Reading programme, science club, debate club and environmental club.
